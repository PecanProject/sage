# Check

Check mode works like Google Docs suggestions. The PDF is on the left and a 380px margin sits on its right, inside the same scroll container, so the cards move with the paper. Each card lines up with its own highlight (the one the PDF scrolls to), and the active card sits exactly beside its highlight while the others move out of its way. Three or more items on one table form a "Table 1 → 22 observations · 6 variables" card that reviews the table a row at a time: what every value shares, then the row's header and reading with ‹ › and Accept row, then each cell, the paper's text beside what goes in the export, the open one with its record under it. Items the paper can't place form a "Not located" cluster at the top. Selecting an item scrolls its highlight and card to the middle of the pane, and every other highlight fades. The zoom is manual and holds across items. Each decision and comment is appended to the paper's `reviews.jsonl`. Data mode is the export workbook: its tabs, rows and columns, with a side sheet per row, row picking for one shared decision, and Ask SAGE.

## Sub-features

- `check-header` is the margin's sticky header: a sage progress bar with "N of M reviewed", the To check / All filter (`filter=all`), and "Accept N SAGE checked". It reads "All checked" with an Export menu when nothing is left.
- `check-cards` are one 32px line per collapsed card, with a status shape, the label and the problem in a few words ("Year is a guess"). Reviewed items show a check or a cross.
- `check-pin` lines each card up with its first highlight, in page, y, then column order. The active card snaps to its highlight, the others move with an 8px gap and a 150ms animation, and nothing overlaps.
- `check-open` expands the active card as the review's Sketch A: `item-place` ("Observation"; the tab only when it is named for something else), `source-excerpt` (the sentence the open field's quote comes from, else the value's), then the record (`record-grid`): one `sheet-field` button per export column, two to a row, labelled with the column's name. A pair opens `field-detail` under its row with its input, the problem when it says more than "needs a look", any quotes the excerpt doesn't show, a "Page N" link and a `fact-comment` link; the first pair that needs a look opens on its own. The footer is Accept, Reject (asks why, with `reasons`), Comment (`sheet-comment`) and More (`sheet-more`: `sheet-edit`, `sheet-source`). Comments list under `comments`.
- `check-clusters` covers the table card (`table-rows`: `table-row` with `row-position`, `row-prev`/`row-next`, `accept-row`, and `row-cell` lines, the open one with `data-active` and `cluster-open` under it; "Also on this table"; "Accept table"; arrow keys), and "Not located" ("Accept all").
- `check-hover` links a card and its highlights: hovering either marks the other.
- `check-pdf` scrolls to the source, outlines a table, and fades the other highlights. It never changes the zoom. The reader zooms with `+`, `-`, Ctrl or ⌘ with the wheel, or a pinch, and `0` or "Fit page" (top right of the PDF pane while zoomed) goes back to Fit page.
- `check-keys` binds j/k to move in document order (the highlight and its open card land centred together), a to accept, e to edit, r to reject with a reason, m to comment, c to change a reviewed item, Shift+A to accept a whole cluster, / to flip the filter, `+`, `-` and `0` to zoom, and ? for help. The key hint at the bottom of the margin hides after ten key presses.
- `check-good` accepts every unreviewed SAGE-checked item it counts (the whole run in Check, the open tab in Data) in one batch review, with a toast "Accepted N items".
- `check-ask` is the Ask SAGE composer (`ask-sage`) at the margin foot and at the bottom of Data mode. `ask-comments-toggle` shows the comment count and opens `ask-comments`, one `ask-comment` per distinct comment with the records it is on. Sending starts a new pass at once on the run in view; `ask-settings` beside Send names what it runs with and opens a popover to change it.
- `check-write` appends each decision to `$SAGE_HOME/papers/<paperId>/.sage/reviews.jsonl`.
- `data-mode` shows the workbook from `GET /api/runs/<run>/workbook`: tabs (`data-tab-<tab>`, `tab=`, Observations first), review columns by default and `all-columns` (`cols=all`) for every one, headers named as the workbook names them. A row click, Enter or `item=` opens the 440px sheet. The open row scrolls into view beside the sheet and clear of the composer. j/k move between rows.
- `data-pick` picks rows (`row-pick`, `x`, ⌘-click, shift-click for a range) into `selection-bar`: Accept, Reject with one reason, Comment, Ask SAGE.
- `check-cli` records a decision with `sage review <run> <record>[,<record>…] accept|edit|reject|comment` (a comment needs `--note`), or `sage review <run> --accept-looks-good`. `sage workbook <run> --json` prints what Data mode shows.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open a paper that has data. It opens in Check mode on the first item that needs a look.
- Choose Check, Data or Read in the top bar's mode switch.
- Click a highlight in Read mode, or a field's "Page N" link in Data mode's sheet, to land in Check on that item.

## Driving it with verify-browser and cli

Preconditions:

- A finished run with records. This needs a real key (see extract-and-watch). Note its `<runId>` and a table group, for example `p7.t1` on Felipe.

- **Margin.** `browser_navigate $SAGE_VERIFY_URL/papers/Felipe-2010-Cultivar`. `[data-testid=margin]` has `data-filter=check`. `[data-testid=review-progress]` reads "0 of N reviewed". Each `[data-testid=card]` carries `data-kind` (`item`, `table` or `unlocated`) and `data-unit`. The open one has `data-active` and holds `[data-testid=item-detail]`.
- **Pinned.** The active card's top is within a few px of `[data-testid=highlight][data-active]`'s top, and that highlight sits about 30% down `[data-testid=pdf-pane]`. No two `[data-testid=margin] section` boxes overlap.
- **PDF follows.** Other highlights carry `data-dim`. `[data-testid=pdf-pane]` carries `data-zoom` (1 at Fit page); pressing `=` or `-` steps it and puts `?zoom=` in the URL, and `0` or `browser_click [data-testid=fit-page]` brings it back to 1. Moving between items never changes it.
- **Hover.** Hover a collapsed card. Its `[data-testid=card]` gains `data-hover`, and its highlights gain `data-hover` too.
- **Accept.** `browser_press_key a`. `[data-testid=review-progress]` rises by one, and the URL's `item=` moves to the next item still to check.
- **Edit.** `browser_press_key e` (or More › Edit). The first problem field's `[data-testid=field-detail]` opens and its `[data-testid=sheet-input]` has focus. Type a value: `[data-testid=sheet-save]` appears. Press `Enter`.
- **Reject.** `browser_press_key r`. `[data-testid=reasons]` lists reasons; click one: it fills `[data-testid=sheet-note]` and focus returns there, so `Enter` rejects.
- **Comment.** `browser_press_key m`, type into `[data-testid=comment-input]`, press `Enter`. The comment shows under `[data-testid=comments]`, and the item stays to check. Hover it: `comment-edit` turns it into `comment-edit-input` (Enter saves, the line in `reviews.jsonl` changes in place), and `comment-remove` drops its lines from every record it was on, with a "Comment removed" toast. `sage comment <paper> <id> "text"` and `sage comment <paper> <id> --remove` do the same.
- **Table.** `browser_navigate …/papers/Felipe-2010-Cultivar?mode=check&item=obs-p7-t1-r2-c3&filter=all`. `[data-testid=table-rows]` starts with `crop processing tomato · statname SE · source Table 1, p. 7`; `[data-testid=table-row]` reads "35 · PAR intercepted (%)", `row-position` "1 / 11", then `variable par_intercepted_pct · reported_units % · n 24`. Its two `row-cell`s read "Fallow mean ± SE … 20±1.0 a" (`treatment_id fallow · value 20 · stat 1`) and "Mustard mean ± SE … 15±0.9 b", the open one with `data-active` and its record (`treatment_id`, `value`, `stat` up front) in `cluster-open`. `row-next` moves `item=` to row 2's first cell and reads "2 / 11". `[data-testid=table-outline]` is drawn on the PDF. Press `ArrowRight` and `ArrowUp`, and `item=` moves to the neighbouring cell. `accept-row` accepts the row's unreviewed cells; `accept-table` (or `Shift+A`) accepts every unreviewed cell.
- **Quoted words.** With a prose item active, its `[data-testid=highlight][data-active]` carries `data-precise` and holds `[data-testid=quote-line]` spans, one per line its quotes cover. `GET /api/runs/<runId>/records` has those boxes under `boxes[<anchor>].quotes[<quote>]`, and the run folder has `quotes.json`. Felipe's `obs-hue-fallow` marks "Fruit were redder … P <0.05" on page 6, not the whole paragraph.
- **Filter.** `browser_press_key /`. The URL gains `filter=all`, and reviewed items appear with `data-reviewed`.
- **Accept SAGE checked.** `browser_click [data-testid=accept-looks-good]`. A toast reads "Accepted N items". In Data mode it accepts only the open tab's.
- **Not located.** A run whose evidence cites anchors the paper doesn't have shows `[data-testid=card][data-kind=unlocated]` first in the margin. Opening one of its items scrolls the pane to the top.
- **Done.** Once nothing needs a look, `[data-testid=all-checked]` shows in the header with `[data-testid=done-export]`.
- **Data mode.** `browser_click [data-testid=mode-data]`. `[data-testid=item-table]` has `data-tab=observations`, and its columns are `sage workbook <run> --json`'s observation review columns that some row fills. Click a `[data-testid=data-row]`: `[data-testid=item-sheet]` opens with the row's facts as `[data-testid=sheet-field]` lines, matching its cells, and the row has `data-selected`. `[data-testid=all-columns]` adds every workbook column and `cols=all` to the URL.
- **Pick and act.** Click a row's `[data-testid=row-pick]`, then shift-click another row: `[data-testid=picked-count]` reads "N selected". `[data-testid=picked-comment]`, type into `[data-testid=picked-input]`, `Enter`: N comment lines land in `reviews.jsonl` and each row shows `[data-testid=row-comments]`. Do the same with `picked-reject` (a reason) and `picked-accept`.
- **Ask SAGE.** With comments left, `browser_click [data-testid=ask-comments-toggle]`: `[data-testid=ask-comments]` lists each distinct comment once, with its records. `[data-testid=ask-settings]`, beside Send, names the run's agent or model; clicking it opens the "Extract with" and effort selects. Don't send unless the run may spend: `Enter` in `[data-testid=ask-input]` starts a run at once, and `run.json` of the new run holds the guidance.
- **Trace.** For one observation, its highlight in Check, its card's facts, its Data row and its row in `sage export <run> --format csv` (`observations.csv`) show the same treatment_id, crop, variable, value, reported_units, statname, stat, n and source. A rejected record is in Data (with its mark) but not the export; a comment shows in the export's `review` column. Open a late row by URL (`?mode=data&item=<an observation near the end>`): the row is inside its scroll box and left of the sheet. `[data-testid=sheet-page]` (inside an expanded section; click a section header first when all are collapsed) returns to Check on that item. Narrow the window until the tabs don't fit (`browser_resize`, no narrower than 768px, where the app stops). The tab row then scrolls sideways inside itself, and `document.scrollingElement.scrollWidth` equals `clientWidth`, so the page never scrolls sideways. If they fit even at 768px, say so.
- **Reload.** After the key actions, reload. The URL's `item=` stays, `review-progress` reads the same, and with `filter=all` each reviewed item carries `data-reviewed` (`accept`, `edit`, `reject`) from the server. Comments don't count as reviewed.
- **On disk.** `tail -3 $SAGE_HOME/papers/<paperId>/.sage/reviews.jsonl` shows the accept, edit (with `field` and `value`), reject (with `note`) and comment (with `note`, and `field` when on one) lines, each with the run and record ids.
- **CLI.** `$S/cli "$root" review review <runId> <recordId> accept --json` exits 0. `$S/cli "$root" good review <runId> --accept-looks-good --json` returns one accept per unreviewed "looks good" item. A second call returns `[]`.
- **Proof.** `evidence/ui/check-item-{light,dark}.png` with an item open, `evidence/ui/check-table-{light,dark}.png` with the table cluster open, `evidence/ui/data-sheet-{light,dark}.png` with a late row open, `evidence/ui/data-picked-{light,dark}.png` with rows picked, and the `reviews.jsonl` tail.

## Gotchas

- Keys typed into a field's input or the note belong to it. Enter saves, and Escape drops that field's draft or closes the note.
- The PDF follows only after the PDF has loaded. Wait for a `[data-testid=highlight]` before you read scroll positions.
- In To check, a card leaves the margin once reviewed (a table cluster stays whole). Use `filter=all` to see the marks, and to find a table cluster whose cells are all reviewed.
- A paper opens on the first item to check without `item=` in the URL. The URL gains `item=` on the first j, k or action.
- The Data mode table's columns are the union of the type's fields, so a row can have fewer sheet sections than the table has columns. Compare sections to the record's own `fields`.
- The key hint counts real key presses in `localStorage["sage.key-hint.uses"]`. Remove that key to see the hint again.
- Without a real key there are no records, and the paper opens in Read mode with the Extract card. Report "needs a real key", not a pass.
